# Sample notes: Intro psychology, memory
(Made-up demo notes. Replace with your own.)

- Memory has three stages: **encoding** (taking info in), **storage** (keeping it), **retrieval** (getting it back).
- **Short-term memory** holds about 7 plus or minus 2 items for roughly 20 to 30 seconds without rehearsal.
- **Long-term memory** is split into explicit (facts and events) and implicit (skills, habits, priming).
- The **testing effect**: practicing retrieval (quizzing yourself) improves long-term retention more than rereading.
- The **spacing effect**: studying spread over several sessions beats cramming the same total time.
- The **forgetting curve** (Ebbinghaus) shows memories fade fastest right after learning unless reviewed.
- The **hippocampus** is key for forming new explicit long-term memories.
