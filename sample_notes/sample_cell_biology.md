# Sample notes: Cell biology
(Made-up demo notes. Replace with your own.)

- The **cell membrane** is a phospholipid bilayer that controls what enters and leaves the cell.
- **Mitochondria** make ATP through cellular respiration, the cell's main energy currency.
- **Ribosomes** build proteins by reading mRNA. They're found free in the cytoplasm or on the rough ER.
- **Prokaryotes** (like bacteria) have no nucleus; **eukaryotes** keep their DNA inside a nucleus.
- **Osmosis** is water moving across a membrane from low solute concentration to high solute concentration.
- **Chloroplasts** in plant cells run photosynthesis, turning light, water and CO2 into glucose and oxygen.
