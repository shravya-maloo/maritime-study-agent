# Sample notes: Calculus, derivatives
(Made-up demo notes. Replace with your own.)

- The **derivative** f'(x) is the instantaneous rate of change of f at x, the slope of the tangent line.
- Limit definition: f'(x) = lim (h -> 0) of [f(x + h) - f(x)] / h.
- **Power rule:** d/dx of x^n is n * x^(n-1). So d/dx of x^3 is 3x^2.
- **Product rule:** (fg)' = f'g + fg'.
- **Chain rule:** d/dx of f(g(x)) is f'(g(x)) * g'(x). Example: d/dx of sin(3x) is 3cos(3x).
- If f'(x) > 0 on an interval, f is increasing there; if f'(x) < 0, f is decreasing.
- **Critical points** are where f'(x) = 0 or f'(x) is undefined; local max/min can only happen there (or at endpoints).
